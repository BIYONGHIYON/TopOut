EfficientDet-Lite2

TopOut uses the Google MediaPipe EfficientDet-Lite2 float16 object detection model
for on-device person location detection.

Model source:
https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/object_detector/efficientdet_lite2/float16/latest/efficientdet_lite2.tflite

The model is used only on device and is downloaded during the Gradle build.
