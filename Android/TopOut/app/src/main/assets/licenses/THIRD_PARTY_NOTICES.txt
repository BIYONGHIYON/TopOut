The MobileSAM model components obtained from the official MobileSAM repository

MoveNet SinglePose Lightning FP16 (version 4)

TopOut uses Google's MoveNet SinglePose Lightning model in the official
TensorFlow Lite FP16 format for on-device human pose estimation.

Official model:
https://tfhub.dev/google/lite-model/movenet/singlepose/lightning/tflite/float16/4

Official TensorFlow MoveNet documentation:
https://www.tensorflow.org/hub/tutorials/movenet

License:
Apache License 2.0

Integration notice:
TopOut performs square crop/padding, uint8 RGB preprocessing, TensorFlow Lite
inference, COCO 17-keypoint mapping, and single-person temporal smart cropping
inside the Android application. Camera images and detected keypoints remain on
the device.


TensorFlow Lite runtime
